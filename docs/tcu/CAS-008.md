## Objective and boundary

Reading stays free. A paid supporter tier and one-off crypto donations exist, and neither gates
anything a reader needs. Payment is a support mechanism, not access control.

In scope: the fiat provider decision, made without vendor allegiance at S0, with provider-hosted
checkout so no card data ever touches the service; webhook handling with signature
verification, idempotency and append-only supporter status; a donation page showing the owner's
crypto addresses, pinned in a policy file the way the workbook digest is; audit events; the
tests at step 9.

Out of scope: any on-chain contract; the hackathon's x402 payer agent, whose disposition is
CAS-006's; cards.

## Acceptance

- A reader starts a subscription through provider-hosted checkout and returns as a supporter
  after the webhook.
- A replayed webhook changes nothing; a forged webhook is refused and logged as a fixed code.
- The donation page shows the pinned addresses and nothing else; changing an address requires
  a commit.
- A supporter and a free reader see the same content unless the owner decided a grant.
