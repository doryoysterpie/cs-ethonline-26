## Objective and boundary

A reader picks stories and receives a shareable card ("these stories caught my interest") for
social media. The owner's reference is the way a well-made, easily shared moment makes a product
sticky.

The owner designs the creatives. This unit builds the functionality only, and only when
admitted. Nothing is specified beyond the decisions below; this tracker exists so the idea is
not lost.
