# Sprint board

Sprints 0 to 9 for ETHOnline 2026, re-cut on 4 September 2026 against the official event
schedule (decision D14). All times are America/Toronto. The controls in the first table are
fixed. Gates are states: a sprint closes when its gate passes. The submission deadline is a
date, and the calendar below is cut so that every gate lands before it.

Note on the gate's contents: the charter's must-ship list numbers the editable draft as
item 5 and MCP tooling as item 6. The re-cut below schedules both inside Sprint 6, whose
window straddles the gate. The gate check on the evening of 10 September therefore counts
items 1 to 4 complete and items 5 and 6 in progress on the Sprint 6 plan. If the project
owner wants items 5 and 6 fully complete at the gate, Sprint 6 must start on 9 September.

## Standing rules

- Sponsor integrations (Sprint 7) begin only after the Graph release candidate passes and
  only if the time budget allows.
- No dashboard polish while live Graph access, clean MCP installation or evidence provenance
  is broken.
- Claude implements on sprint branches. Codex independently reviews diffs, installs locked
  dependencies when appropriate, and reruns verification. Neither agent merges to `main`
  without the project owner's instruction. Sprint 1 does not begin before the Codex audit of
  Sprint 0 is answered.
